1. Information We Collect

InstallIQ collects the following information in order to provide scheduling and installation management services:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Business name
  • Service address
  • Work order details
  • Installation photos uploaded through the platform

We may also collect usage data related to platform access and messaging activity.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Schedule and manage installation services
  • Send appointment confirmations and scheduling updates
  • Send installer arrival notifications
  • Send secure upload links for subcontractors
  • Provide customer support
  • Maintain service records

We do not use phone numbers for marketing unless explicitly authorized.

3. SMS Communications

By providing your phone number through InstallIQ during service scheduling or account registration, you consent to receive SMS messages related to:

  • Appointment confirmations
  • Scheduling updates
  • Installer arrival notifications
  • Work order updates
  • Secure upload links

Message frequency varies based on service activity.

Message and data rates may apply.

You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message.

For assistance, reply HELP or contact us using the information below.

4. Consent

SMS consent is collected during service booking or account registration.

Consent is recorded and stored within our system.

SMS consent is not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.

5. Data Sharing

InstallIQ does not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Information may be shared only:

  • With installation teams or subcontractors for service fulfillment
  • With service providers necessary to operate the platform (e.g., cloud hosting, messaging providers)
  • When required by law

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information.

7. Data Retention

We retain service records and related communications as necessary to provide services and maintain business records.
